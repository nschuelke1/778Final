Dam Failure Risk Viewer

What happens when the unexpected becomes reality?

This interactive map helps you explore potential impacts of dam failure across Wisconsin. Whether you're a resident, planner, or emergency responder, understanding flood risk is key to preparedness.

  • Buffer Tool: Visualize zones around critical infrastructure.
  • Elevation Tool: Estimate flood reach based on terrain.
  • Summary Panel: See affected schools, hospitals, property value and size.

Use the map to explore your area, toggle layers, and simulate different scenarios. You can use the Dam Buffer tool by clicking the button, choosing your distance and clicking a dam. You can also use the Compare Elevation tool by clicking the button and choosing 2 points on the map. When you are finnished click Clear Results and start again! Knowledge is the first step toward resilience.
